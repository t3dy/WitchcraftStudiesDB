WitchcraftStudiesDB

A relational knowledge portal for the history of European and transatlantic witch trials, 1428–1692. Built on three scholarly traditions: Stuart Clark (intellectual history), Ronald Hutton (social history), and Carlo Ginzburg (microhistory).

About the Database

WitchcraftStudiesDB integrates three scholarly frameworks for the history of witchcraft persecution. Entries are sourced directly from primary documents and peer-reviewed secondary literature. Every claim carries provenance: a named source, confidence level, and statement of scholarly disagreement where it exists.

Navigate using the menu above. Use Timeline for chronological overview, Trials for individual prosecutions, Scholars for intellectual figures, and Concepts for analytical categories.
